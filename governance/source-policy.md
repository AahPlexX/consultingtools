# Evidence and Source Policy

## Evidence standard

Consulting conclusions must be proportionate to the quality and freshness of their evidence. Distinguish source facts, calculations, user-supplied assumptions, model inferences, and recommendations.

## Freshness

Use current research when a material fact can change over time, including laws, regulations, prices, company information, market conditions, product capabilities, software/API behavior, search-engine guidance, and industry metrics. Record or communicate the relevant as-of date when it affects interpretation.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. Primary authoritative sources: official regulators, standards bodies, audited/official company material, first-party product documentation, official datasets, original filings, and primary research.
2. High-quality independent sources with transparent methodology when primary sources are insufficient or when independent corroboration is needed.
3. Secondary aggregators only when necessary and clearly identified as such.

Do not use a lower-quality source merely because it is easier to access. Do not treat search-result snippets as evidence when the underlying source can be inspected.

## Corroboration

Corroborate material claims when a single source is incomplete, conflicted, commercially interested, methodologically weak, or not authoritative for the claim. When reliable sources disagree, preserve the disagreement and explain what can and cannot be concluded.

## Metrics and estimates

- Never invent SEO volumes, backlink counts, traffic, conversion rates, market sizes, financial figures, benchmarks, or other quantitative evidence.
- Label modeled values and estimates; provide assumptions, formulas, units, date basis, and sensitivity where material.
- Distinguish measured data from forecasts and scenarios.
- Avoid false precision. Round outputs to a level justified by the input quality.

## Citation and provenance

A user-facing report that relies on external evidence should make the material evidence traceable to its source. Generated artifacts should retain citation/provenance information in a format appropriate to that artifact. Internal tool output should include stable source identifiers or user-openable URLs when supported.

## Research completion

Research is complete only when the evidence is sufficient to answer the decision-relevant question, major contradictions have been investigated, material gaps are disclosed, and further searching is unlikely to change the conclusion enough to justify the cost or delay.
